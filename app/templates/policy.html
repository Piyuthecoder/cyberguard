Privacy notice (v{{ config.POLICY_VERSION }})

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. This is a project template and must be reviewed by a legal expert before production use.

What we collect and why

  • Account data (name, email, optional phone): to identify you and send one-time passcodes.
  • Complaint data (narrative, suspect identifiers such as UPI IDs and phone numbers, uploaded evidence): to register and investigate your complaint.
  • Audit records (who did what, when, from which IP address): to protect the chain of custody of evidence.

Who can see it

You, and investigating officers and administrators of this portal. Every officer view or download of evidence is logged.

How long we keep it

Complaint data is kept while the case is open and for {{ config.RETENTION_YEARS }} years after closure, then erased.

Your rights

  • Access and download your data from My data.
  • Withdraw consent and request erasure. If a complaint is still under investigation, erasure is deferred until it closes, because the law allows data needed for investigation to be retained.
  • Erasure removes personal data and evidence files; the audit log keeps only non-personal fingerprints (hashes) as proof that evidence existed.
